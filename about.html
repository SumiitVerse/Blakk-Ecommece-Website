✦ Our Origin
The Story

Born from a Belief That
Excellence is Non-Negotiable

In 2015, our founder Aryan Mehta was styling a photo shoot in a cramped Mumbai studio, manually managing email lists on a spreadsheet, and wondering why the two worlds — fashion and digital marketing — were so stubbornly separate.

He believed that the brands winning the next decade wouldn't be those with the biggest budgets, but those who understood both the art of luxury and the science of digital acquisition. BLAKK was the answer to that belief.

From a team of three operating out of a Bandra apartment, we've grown into a 60-person powerhouse with offices in Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore — serving premium labels across India, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East.
